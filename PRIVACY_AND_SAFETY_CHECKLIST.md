# Pre-publication privacy and safety checklist

Before uploading the guide publicly:

- [ ] Confirm screenshots do not expose the user's full name.
- [ ] Confirm screenshots do not expose Wi-Fi passwords.
- [ ] Confirm personal network names are redacted or intentionally retained.
- [ ] Confirm Bluetooth MAC addresses / hardware addresses are redacted.
- [ ] Confirm local usernames and home-folder paths are redacted where appropriate.
- [ ] Confirm serial numbers, UUIDs, and other unique device identifiers are redacted.
- [ ] Confirm no email address, phone number, physical address, or account token appears.
- [ ] Confirm no browser tabs reveal private services or logged-in accounts.
- [ ] Confirm destructive `diskutil` commands are accompanied by a warning that disk identifiers are machine-specific.
- [ ] Confirm the guide is labeled as an independent case study, not official OCLP documentation.
- [ ] Compare the guide with current official OCLP documentation before publication.
- [ ] Choose and add a license if you want others to redistribute or adapt the guide.
